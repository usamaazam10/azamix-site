{% include section-head.html no="03" label="Editorial standards" id="standards-title" title="How we write." %}

  1. Useful first

    Every article should help a reader understand something or make a better decision. We don't publish pieces written only to rank in search results.

  2. Sourced and dated

    Facts that can change, such as prices, platform rules and product features, are linked to their source. Articles show when they were published and when they were last substantively updated.

  3. No invented evidence

    We don't make up statistics, quotes, test results or experiences. Where something is our opinion or a general pattern, we say so.

  4. Clear about interests

    Azamix sells software development, and articles end with a note saying so. If we ever publish sponsored or paid content, it will be clearly labelled as such.
