Operations dashboards and reporting

Every order, approval and stock level in one view. Numbers come straight from the records, so nobody spends Friday afternoon rebuilding a spreadsheet, and everyone works from the same version of the truth.

Typically includes

  • A data model built around your business
  • Roles and permissions for each team
  • Approval steps, alerts and reminders
  • Reports and exports on demand

Often connects to

  • Accounting
  • E-commerce
  • Spreadsheets (migration)
  • Email

Job and workflow management

Work moves through clear stages, from scheduled to done, with the office and the field looking at the same jobs. Staff on site capture photos, notes and signatures on their phones; the office sees it the moment they do.

Typically includes

  • Status boards and scheduling
  • A mobile app for staff on site
  • Photos, checklists and signatures
  • History of who did what, and when

Often connects to

  • Calendars
  • Maps
  • Invoicing
  • Customer notifications

Customer and partner portals

Give customers a place to help themselves: invoices, orders, documents and requests, available any time. Fewer emails and phone calls for your team, and a better experience for the people you serve.

Typically includes

  • Secure sign-in for each customer
  • Invoices, orders and documents
  • Requests that reach the right person
  • An admin view for your team

Often connects to

  • Accounting
  • CRM
  • File storage

05 / 06

AI document processing

Invoices, forms and emails read automatically and turned into clean data. Anything uncertain goes to a review queue, so a person checks what matters and your systems only receive what's right.

Typically includes

  • Extraction tested on your own documents
  • Validation rules and a review queue
  • Routing into your existing systems
  • Logs, accuracy tracking and cost monitoring

Often connects to

  • Email inboxes
  • Accounting
  • Document storage
  • Hosted AI models

Connected systems and integrations

Your apps, internal tools and the services you already pay for, all working from one source of truth. Data moves automatically, failures retry safely, and there's a record of every sync.

Typically includes

  • Documented APIs for your products
  • Two-way sync between systems
  • Safe retries and duplicate protection
  • Alerts when something needs attention

Often connects to

  • CRM
  • Accounting
  • E-commerce
  • Payments
