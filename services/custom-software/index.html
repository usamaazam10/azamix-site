01Problems it solves

Where this usually starts.

  • Work lives in spreadsheets.

    Data is copied between files and there's no single source of truth. A custom business system can bring it into one place.

  • Your tools don't talk to each other.

    Staff re-enter the same information in several places. Integrations between systems can remove that duplicate work.

  • Repetitive manual tasks.

    Reports, approvals, and reminders depend on someone remembering them. Many of these predictable steps can be automated.

  • Off-the-shelf software doesn't fit.

    You've bent your process around a product that wasn't built for it. Custom software is built around the process instead.

02What it covers

What we build.

The exact scope depends on your project.

  • Enterprise and business systemsSystems for running the business, such as HR, accounts, inventory, and analytics modules.
  • Automation platformsSoftware that automates repetitive business processes.
  • IntegrationsConnections between your systems through APIs.

03Fit

Is this the right service?

A good fit when

  • Your processes are specific to your business and central to how you operate
  • Staff spend significant time on manual data entry or copying between tools
  • You need several existing systems connected

Probably not needed when

  • An established off-the-shelf product already covers your needs well
  • You need a throwaway tool for a short, one-time task
