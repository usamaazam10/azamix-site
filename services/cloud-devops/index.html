01Problems it solves

Where this usually starts.

  • Deployments are manual.

    Releases depend on someone running steps by hand. CI/CD pipelines automate builds and deployment.

  • Users find problems before you do.

    Without monitoring, outages are discovered late. Monitoring and alerts surface issues sooner.

  • The current setup can't keep up.

    Traffic or data has outgrown the existing servers. Cloud infrastructure can scale with demand.

  • You're moving to the cloud.

    Migrating existing systems needs careful planning so nothing breaks along the way.

02What it covers

What we build.

The exact scope depends on your project.

  • CI/CD pipelinesAutomated build and deployment for your applications.
  • Cloud migrationMoving existing systems to the cloud on AWS.
  • ContainerisationDocker and Kubernetes for packaging and running services.
  • Automation & scalabilityInfrastructure that is automated and scales with demand.
  • MonitoringVisibility into how your systems are running.

03Fit

Is this the right service?

A good fit when

  • Releases are slow or manual
  • You're planning a move to AWS
  • Your system has outgrown a single server
  • You need visibility into errors and performance

Probably not needed when

  • A managed hosting platform already handles your deploys without trouble
  • Your application is small enough that a simple hosting plan is enough
