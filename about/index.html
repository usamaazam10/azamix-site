About Azamix

Technology worth knowing. Software worth building.

Azamix does two things. We design and build software for businesses: custom systems, mobile apps, web platforms, AI features and integrations. And we publish clear explanations of the technology behind them, for anyone who has to make decisions about it.

{% include section-head.html no="01" label="Philosophy" id="why-title" title="Why build and explain." %}

Most businesses don't need more technology. They need the right piece of it, built well, and a clear explanation of why.

That's the idea behind Azamix. On the services side, we build software that solves a specific problem: a workflow that takes too long, systems that don't share data, customers who need a better way to reach you. On the publishing side, we explain how technology actually works and where it's genuinely useful, without the hype that makes good decisions harder.

Each side keeps the other honest. Building real systems keeps the writing grounded in what works in practice. Explaining technology in plain language keeps the engineering disciplined: if we can't explain why a system is built a certain way, it probably shouldn't be built that way.

Useful over impressive.

We'd rather recommend a simple, proven approach than an exciting one. That means using established technology that others can maintain, telling you when an existing product would serve you better than custom software, and treating AI as a tool to apply carefully rather than a goal in itself.

It also means taking the unglamorous parts seriously: permissions, testing, documentation, backups and maintenance. They're what decide whether software is still useful in three years.
