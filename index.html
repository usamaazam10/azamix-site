How we work

Four steps from first call to launch day.

  1. Discuss

    Bring the problem, not a specification. We talk through how the work happens today, who will use the software and what it needs to connect to.

  2. Define

    We agree what the first version must do, what can wait and how it will be built. You get a written scope and an estimate before any code is written.

  3. Build

    You see working software early and often, not just status reports. Your feedback shapes each stage, and any change to scope is your decision.

  4. Deliver

    Tested, deployed and documented. You get the code, the access and the know-how, plus ongoing support if you want it.

What working with us is like

  • Your requirements firstSoftware shaped by how your business works. If an existing product would serve you better, we'll say so.
  • No surprisesA clear scope, working builds you can try, and risks raised the moment they appear.
  • Proven technologyEstablished, well-supported tools, chosen for your problem and for whoever maintains the software next.
  • Built to lastTested logic, careful handling of data and access, and documentation that travels with the code.
