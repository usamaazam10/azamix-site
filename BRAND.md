# Azamix brand identity

> Understand what's next. Build what matters.

## The mark

The symbol is a geometric lowercase **a** built from two parts:

- **The frame**: a single rounded corner, like the corner of a page, a screen or a crop mark. It stands for editorial framing (choosing what matters and explaining it) and for the structure of software we build.
- **The point**: a teal disc held inside the frame. It stands for the idea in focus: the story, the product, what's next.

The frame and the point read together as one letter, the brand's initial. They show the two sides of Azamix (media and software) as one system, without stacking literal icons. The disc's radius matches the frame's outer corner radius (8 units on a 32-unit grid), and the stroke is 6 units, so the mark holds up at 16 px.

The wordmark is lowercase `azamix`, set in Geist SemiBold, tightened, and converted to outlines. The dot of the **i** is the same teal disc as the symbol, so the two parts of the logo also work apart.

## Usage

- **Clear space:** keep at least half the symbol's height clear on every side.
- **Minimum size:** the lockup at 20 px tall on screen, the symbol at 16 px. Below 24 px, use the app-icon tile.
- **Ground:** use the light file on warm white or white and the dark file on navy. On photos or busy colour, use a mono file.
- **Don't** add a descriptor such as "Software Solutions" inside the lockup, recolour the parts, re-space the wordmark, add effects, or rotate the mark. To name a division, set it as separate text next to the logo, divided by a rule.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight navy (`--ink`) | `#101923` | Primary: text, dark grounds, the logo frame |
| Warm white (`--paper`) | `#F7F8F6` | Editorial ground, the logo on dark |
| Teal (`--teal`) | `#74D4C3` | Signature accent on navy. 10:1 against navy |
| Mid teal (`--teal-mid`) | `#23A58F` | Logo accent on light grounds, where the signature teal is too pale |
| Deep teal (`--accent`) | `#0B7466` | Links, buttons and focus on light grounds. 5.3:1 against warm white, safe for text |

Teal is one hue in three steps. On a light ground, use the deeper step: `#74D4C3` on warm white is only 1.65:1.

## Type

Geist for headlines and text, Geist Mono for labels and data (both self-hosted, SIL OFL). Headlines are medium or semibold with tight tracking. This works for an editorial feature and for a services page.

## Note

The mark is original work, but no trademark search has been run. Before registering it or investing in printed material, have a trademark search done in Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan) and in your main international markets (WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO, EUIPO).
